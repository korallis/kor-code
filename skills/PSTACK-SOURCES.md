# pstack attribution

The adaptations listed in `pstack.lock.json` derive from
[cursor/plugins, pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/e5136d82418d2db3165ea1050d17577cd618cc05/pstack)
at commit `e5136d82418d2db3165ea1050d17577cd618cc05`.
The source is MIT licensed, copyright (c) 2026 Lauren Tan. Every derived
bundle includes the complete notice in `LICENSE.upstream`.

The manifest records the upstream file, its SHA-256, the adapted `SKILL.md`
SHA-256, and the upstream license path and SHA-256. It covers 22 principles,
eight supporting skills, and nine session playbooks. `change-docs` derives
from the technical-writing skill. `bespoke-playbook` and `sketch-types` derive
from figure-it-out and architect. The other six playbooks derive from the
corresponding poteto-mode procedures.

Kor adaptations remove editor commands, role defaults, dispatch instructions,
shipping supervision, and requirements to create tests. They use Kor metadata,
respect repository permissions and test policy, and keep procedures self-contained.
Supporting skills are condensed adaptations rather than complete upstream copies.

`diagnose-bug` and `needs-input-protocol` are original Kor guidance under the
repository's MIT license. They generalize causal diagnosis and authority handling
without distributing operator-specific procedures or state.
