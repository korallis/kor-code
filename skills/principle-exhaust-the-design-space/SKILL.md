# Compare design alternatives

Build two or three structurally distinct prototypes when a novel interaction or interface has no established precedent. Compare them on the actual surface before committing to a direction.

Use observed behavior and the consumer workflow to explain the choice. A settled mechanical change does not need competing prototypes. Keep experiments within the authorized scope and record their limits.
