# Threat model

## Scope and assets

State the design status and list valuable data, operations and identities. Explain
what is outside scope and why.

## Trust boundaries

Identify callers, services, storage and third parties. State who can cross each
boundary and which inputs are untrusted.

## Threats and controls

| Threat | Asset and entry point | Requirement IDs | Mitigation | Verification or planned check | Residual risk and owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concrete misuse or failure | What is at risk and where | Relevant IDs | Enforced or proposed control | Observed evidence or pending work | Remaining exposure and responsible role |

## Open risks

Record missing controls and decisions with owners. Planned controls are not
security evidence. Use synthetic examples without credentials or personal data.
