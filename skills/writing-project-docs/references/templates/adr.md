# NNNN: Decision title

Status: proposed, accepted or superseded; choose the actual state.

## Context

Describe the concrete constraint, requirement IDs and alternatives that made a
decision necessary. An undecided choice remains proposed with an open question.

## Decision

State the choice and its boundary. For accepted decisions, record the evidence or
authority for acceptance. For superseded decisions, identify the replacement.

## Consequences

Describe the benefits, costs and follow-up work, including affected contracts or
risks. Keep this short enough to read before changing the decision.
