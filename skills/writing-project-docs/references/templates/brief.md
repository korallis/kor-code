# Product brief

## Users and problem

Name the users, their current problem and the desired outcome. Preserve supplied
owner wording as a quotation where useful; attribute no invented statements.

## Goals and success measures

For each goal, state an observable success measure and how it will be checked.

## Constraints

Record supplied platform, data, access, delivery and operational constraints.
Separate confirmed constraints from proposals.

## Non-goals

List explicit exclusions. Scope uncertainty belongs in open questions.

## Decisions needed

Identify unanswered choices, their owners, proposed defaults and blocked work.
Use stable question IDs from the open-questions document.
