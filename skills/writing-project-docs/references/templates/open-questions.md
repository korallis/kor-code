# Open questions

| ID | Question | Owner | Proposed default | Blocks | Resolution/status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q-001 | Specific missing fact or decision | Responsible role, or owner unassigned | Proposed answer, or no safe default with reason | Work/requirement IDs | Open or answer with authority |

A proposed default is not an accepted decision. Resolve questions from evidence
or the responsible owner's answer, then update affected requirements, ADRs, specs
and planned work. Keep resolved entries traceable. If there are no open questions,
state that explicitly and record the evidence used to settle supplied unknowns.
